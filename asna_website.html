The Studio

A practice built on
slow making

ASNA Needle & Co. is a one-woman design studio rooted in the belief that handcraft is the most honest form of expression. Every piece made here is stitched by hand, considered over time, and released only when it is ready.

We work at the intersection of football culture and textile art — not as a commercial venture, but as a personal investigation into what sport means when translated through thread, crochet, and paint.

— Asna, Studio Founder
